🗄️ Database Models

Asset

Main file storage with metadata, versioning, and activity tracking

AssetCategory

Organize assets with custom colors, icons, and descriptions

AssetVersion

Complete version history with change logs and rollback

AssetPermission

Role-based access control (Viewer, Editor, Owner)

AssetShare

Shareable links with password, expiration, access levels

AssetActivity

Audit trail of all operations (create, update, delete, share)
