# Row change history

Row change history in Baserow allows you to track and manage changes made to your data over time.

This support article will guide you through how to track row changes.

![Baserow row change history](https://baserow-backend-production20240528124524339000000001.s3.amazonaws.com/pagedown-uploads/a382ed52-2120-46b8-965e-6c4cede5d94c/Row%20change%20history.png)

## Access row change history

To access a row’s history,

  1. Open your Baserow database and navigate to the specific row you want to track changes for.
  2. [Enlarge the selected row](/user-docs/enlarging-rows) to reveal more details.
  3. Within the expanded view, click on the **History** tab to access the row change history.

In the row change history, you will find information about the modifications made to the selected row:

  * **Author:** This displays the user or contributor who made the changes.
  * **Details:** Provides a comprehensive summary of what was changed in the row.
  * **Date and Time:** Shows the timestamp of when the changes were made.

## Baserow plan limitations

The availability of the Row Change History depends on your [Baserow subscription plan](/pricing):

### Baserow Cloud

  * **Free Plan:** You can review changes made within the past 14 days.
  * **Premium Plan:** This plan grants access to changes made within the last 90 days.
  * **Advanced Plan:** Subscribers to the Advanced Plan can view changes made within the previous 180 days.

### Baserow Self-Hosted

  * **Free plan** : Unlimited.
  * **Premium plan** : Unlimited.
  * **Enterprise plan** : Unlimited.

By default, there is a limit of 365 days of revisions for Baserow Self-Hosted, but you have the flexibility to change this limit to any other number.

By keeping track of row changes, you can maintain data integrity, collaborate effectively, and identify any errors or discrepancies in your database.
