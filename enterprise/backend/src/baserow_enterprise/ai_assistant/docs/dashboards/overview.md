## Widgets

![Baserow Dashboard Widgets](https://baserow-backend-production20240528124524339000000001.s3.amazonaws.com/pagedown-uploads/9af9799c-80c3-44fa-8c86-338f87689e42/Baserow%20Dashboards\(2\).png)

The Dashboards feature allows you to create visual representations of your data through various widgets, making it easier to understand trends and make data-driven decisions.

Widgets are the building blocks of dashboards that shape how your data is visualized. Currently available widgets include:

  * **Summary widget:** Displays key metrics like totals, averages, and counts for quick insights at a glance.
  * **Bar chart widget:** Shows data as bar charts or line charts with up to three series per chart, perfect for comparing values and tracking changes over time. You can choose between bar and line series depending on how you want to visualize your data trends.

### Customization options

Each widget can be customized with:

  * Unique titles and descriptions
  * Specific data sources
  * Grouping options
  * Sorting preferences

### Data integration

Dashboards automatically update when your Baserow tables change, ensuring your visualizations always reflect the latest data. You can combine data from multiple databases or tables in a single dashboard for comprehensive analysis.
