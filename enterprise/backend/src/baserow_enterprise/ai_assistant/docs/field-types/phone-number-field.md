# Phone number field

A phone number field will format a string of numbers as a phone number. The phone number field enables you to input phone numbers in a structured format, ensuring that your data is consistent and easy to manage. The phone number field makes it easy to keep track of important contact information and stay connected with your network.

In this section, we will explore the different aspects of the Baserow phone number field.

## How to add a phone number field

To add a phone number field,

  1. Click the plus sign + to the right of your existing fields.
  2. Select the phone number field type and input the name of the field.
  3. Click **Create**.
