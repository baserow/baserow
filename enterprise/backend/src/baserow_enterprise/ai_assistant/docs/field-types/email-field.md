# Email field

Email field type allows users to store and manage email addresses in their databases. With the Email field, users can track and organize contact information and automate email workflows using integrations with third-party tools.

In this section, we will explore the benefits of using the Email field in Baserow, and best practices for setting up and using this field type. Each cell in an email field is designed to hold a single email address.

To add an email field,

  1. Click the plus sign + to the right of your existing fields.
  2. Select the email field type and input the name of the field.
  3. Click **Create**
