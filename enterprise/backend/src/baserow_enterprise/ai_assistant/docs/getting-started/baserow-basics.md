# Introduction to Baserow

In this section, we’ll cover the fundamental components and the essentials of Baserow. We’ll cover everything you need to know about using Baserow to make the best possible decisions for your company.

## Discover Baserow

Baserow is an open-source no-code database platform that allows you to collaborate on projects, tasks, and more. It gives you the powers of a developer without leaving your browser.

Baserow is designed to help you manage and organize your data with ease. Whether you’re handling large datasets, automating processes, or building custom applications, Baserow provides the tools you need without requiring programming knowledge. By streamlining data management and application creation, Baserow empowers teams to work smarter and more efficiently.

## Overview

At its core, Baserow is a collaborative database platform that lets you create, manage, and visualize your data in a way that suits your workflow. With Baserow, you can design custom databases, build applications, and automate tasks, all within a single interface. It’s designed to be flexible and adaptable, making it suitable for a variety of industries and departments.

## Understand how Baserow transforms your work

Baserow enhances productivity by allowing you to customize your workspace according to your needs. Its database builder simplifies data structuring and visualization, while its application builder lets you create tailored applications without any coding. With Baserow, teams can [collaborate](/user-docs/managing-workspace-collaborators) more effectively, reduce manual work, and focus on what matters most—getting things done.

## Database Builder

Baserow’s [Database Builder](/user-docs/intro-to-databases) is the foundation of the platform. It allows you to structure, visualize, and manipulate your data in a way that makes sense for your business. You can create custom tables, define relationships between data, and use powerful filters and views to see your data from different angles. The Database Builder makes it easy to manage everything from simple lists to complex datasets.

## Application Builder

The [Application Builder](/user-docs/application-builder-overview) in Baserow lets you create custom applications without writing a single line of code. Whether you need a project management tool, a CRM, or any other business application, Baserow enables you to build it yourself. The drag-and-drop interface allows you to design workflows, automate tasks, and integrate with other tools, giving you full control over how your applications function.

## Enterprise

For organizations that require robust security, user management, and high performance at scale, Baserow offers [enterprise features](/user-docs/enterprise-license-overview) that ensure your data is protected and your team can work efficiently. With advanced permissions, audit logs, and scalable infrastructure, Baserow Enterprise meets the needs of large businesses and institutions.

## Baserow Cloud

Baserow Cloud is the hosted version of the platform, where we take care of the technical details for you. With Baserow Cloud, you get all the features of Baserow without the need to manage servers or infrastructure. We handle updates, backups, and security, so you can focus on using the platform to drive your business forward.

## Baserow Self-Hosted

If you prefer to keep full control over your data and infrastructure, Baserow Self-Hosted is the option for you. You can host Baserow on your own servers, whether on-premise or in the cloud. This version gives you the flexibility to customize the environment to meet your specific requirements, with the added benefit of knowing exactly where your data is stored.

## API-first

Baserow features unique self-hosting capabilities, a high-performance design to handle very large volumes of data, and an [API-first](https://api.baserow.io/api/redoc/#tag/Workspaces/operation/list_workspaces) design for interoperability with any other tool.

Baserow [integrates](/user-docs/database-api) seamlessly with other tools and platforms, allowing you to extend its functionality and connect it to your existing workflows. Whether you need to connect with external databases, automate tasks with third-party services, or pull in data from other applications, Baserow’s integrations make it possible.

## Departments

Baserow is versatile and can be adapted to meet the needs of different departments within your organization:

  * **Human Resources** : Manage employee data, track recruitment processes, and streamline onboarding.
  * **Marketing** : Organize campaigns, manage leads, and analyze performance.
  * **Information Technology** : Oversee IT assets, manage support tickets, and track software licenses.
  * **Sales** : Track leads, manage customer relationships, and monitor sales performance.
  * **Product** : Plan product development, track feature requests, and manage roadmaps.
  * **Operations** : Streamline processes, manage projects, and track operational metrics.
  * **Finance** : Track budgets, manage expenses, and oversee financial reporting.

## Industries

Baserow is not just for one type of business; it’s flexible enough to be used across various industries:

  * **Manufacturing** : Manage production schedules, track inventory, and monitor supply chains.
  * **Real Estate and Construction** : Track properties, manage construction projects, and oversee budgets.
  * **Financial Services** : Organize client information, track transactions, and manage portfolios.
  * **Healthcare** : Manage patient data, track medical records, and oversee facility operations.
  * **Education** : Organize student data, manage curricula, and track academic progress.
  * **Technology** : Oversee development projects, manage software releases, and track user feedback.
